Dropshipping

Sell from our catalogue without holding stock. You order one piece at a time, after your own customer has bought it from you, and we ship it straight to their address. No minimum, no carton, no warehouse.

This is a trade service, not a consumer shop. The order is placed by a verified trade partner on behalf of their own customer. VESTRA has no contract of sale with that customer — they remain yours, and after-sales is between you and them. The full wording is clause 2a of the Terms of Service.

What it costs

The dropship price of an article is the wholesale price of its smallest quantity tier, plus 20%. The smallest tier is the most expensive one, because volume discounts belong to volume; a single piece does not earn the 300-piece price. The added margin covers picking, packing and invoicing one unit instead of a carton.

🔓 Wholesale access

With a wholesale access plan the 20% single-piece surcharge does not apply: you pay the plain wholesale price on every single-piece order. US$199.90 per month, cancel any time.

It pays for itself once you order regularly; below that, ordering without the plan is cheaper. Both prices are shown on every article before you buy.

What is not available

Most of the catalogue is open to single-piece purchase. These are not:

  • Footwear — the whole section is sold by the box only; single pairs are not picked from assorted cartons.
  • Underwear — the whole section is sold by the box only; single pairs are not picked from assorted cartons.
  • Ralph Lauren — excluded at the supplier's request.
  • Lacoste — excluded at the supplier's request.
  • Boxershorts and boxer briefs — any article whose name or category contains "boxer". Underwear is not returnable, so it is not a sensible single-piece line.

These rules are applied by the catalogue itself, so a listing cannot be opened to dropshipping by mistake. The feed is the authority: read it rather than filtering our brand list yourself.

Availability

Per-unit stock is not tracked for catalogue articles, and we do not invent a number to fill the gap. Availability is confirmed with the seller after your order. If it cannot be met, the order is refunded in full.

This is stated before you pay, and it is the single most important thing to carry into your own shop: do not promise your customer a delivery date from a stock figure you did not get from us, because we did not give you one.

Duties and import taxes

Duties and import taxes at the destination are not included in either the goods price or the shipping rate. They fall due on delivery. You can settle them yourself or leave them to your customer — decide which before you quote, because an unexpected invoice at the door is what makes a parcel get refused.

Goods of EU preferential origin may attract zero customs duty entering Japan under the EU–Japan Economic Partnership Agreement, when a statement on origin accompanies the consignment. That covers the tariff only. It does not cover consumption tax or the carrier's clearance fee.

Photographs and product text

Product photographs are supplied by our sellers and are not VESTRA's property. We are not in a position to grant you a licence to them. If you use them to resell in your own shop, that use and any consequence of it rests with you. Several of our partners photograph their own stock for exactly this reason.

How an order runs

  1. Your customer buys the article from you, at your price.
  2. You open the article on VESTRA and choose the colour, the size and the destination.
  3. You pay by card and enter your customer's delivery address on the payment page.
  4. The seller is notified with that address and ships the piece directly.
  5. You receive the order reference and the invoice. Your customer receives the parcel.

The payment line on the card statement reads Dropshipping · SKU … · Ident. … and carries no brand name, because VESTRA is a wholesale marketplace and not an authorised representative of the houses it carries. The full article detail stays on your order confirmation and invoice.

Doing it through the API

Everything above can be driven from your own shop or ERP. Keys are issued per partner, free of charge, to verified trade accounts. Every request carries an Authorization: Bearer header.

GET  /api/dropship?a=list
GET  /api/dropship?a=stock&id=ARTICLE-ID
POST /api/dropship?a=order

{ "id": "ARTICLE-ID", "colour": "Black", "size": "M", "qty": 1,
  "country": "JP", "reference": "your-order-id",
  "customer_email": "…", "customer_name": "…" }

a=stock returns the price, the colours and sizes the listing knows, and the shipping table above with its transit times — so your integration reads one source instead of copying figures from this page. a=order returns a payment link you open to pay. Pass country as an ISO-2 code and we map it to the right zone for you.

The catalogue feed is separate and read-only: brands, articles, sizes, colours, minimum order quantities, tiered wholesale prices in EUR and photograph URLs, with a since filter so a nightly sync pulls only what changed. It carries no EAN or GTIN and no live stock quantities, said here rather than discovered after you have written an importer.
