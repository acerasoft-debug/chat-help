# VESTRA — AML / KYC Policy

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Demonstrates VESTRA's anti-money-laundering and verification
> approach for provider onboarding. A compliance specialist should tailor this to applicable law.

## 1. Purpose
VESTRA prevents the use of its marketplace for money laundering, sanctions evasion, fraud, or
terrorist financing, and verifies the identity of its business users.

## 2. Verification (KYB/KYC)
Before transacting, **every Seller** (and business Buyer where required) is verified:
- business registration / incorporation document;
- tax / VAT identifier;
- **Ultimate Beneficial Owners (≥25%)** identity (passport / national ID) and address proof;
- business address proof.
VESTRA may re-verify periodically or on risk triggers.

## 3. Sanctions & screening
Users and beneficial owners are screened against applicable **sanctions lists** (e.g., OFAC, EU,
UN). VESTRA does not onboard users in prohibited/sanctioned jurisdictions.

## 4. Fund handling delegated to a licensed provider
All payment collection, holding (escrow), and settlement are performed by a **licensed payment/
escrow institution** (Tazapay or equivalent). VESTRA does **not** hold or transmit user funds and
relies on the provider's regulated AML controls in addition to its own.

## 5. Ongoing monitoring
VESTRA monitors for suspicious patterns (e.g., structuring, unusual volumes, mismatched
identities, repeated disputes) and may pause, investigate, or report as required.

## 6. Record-keeping
Verification records and transaction logs are retained for the period required by applicable law.

## 7. Roles & reporting
[Named compliance contact] is responsible for this policy. Suspicious activity is escalated and
reported to the competent authority / via the licensed provider where applicable.
