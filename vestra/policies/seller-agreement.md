# VESTRA — Seller Agreement

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** This is the document that places product responsibility on the
> Seller and protects VESTRA. Have a lawyer finalise it.

**Between:** [VESTRA LLC] ("VESTRA") and the registered business seller ("Seller").

## 1. Verification (KYB/KYC)
The Seller will provide and keep current: business registration, tax/VAT ID, beneficial-owner
identity, and any documents reasonably required. VESTRA may suspend listings pending verification.

## 2. Seller is the seller of record
The Seller is the **legal seller** of its goods. VESTRA is only an intermediary and is not a party
to the sale. The Seller is responsible for product conformity, safety, delivery, warranties, taxes,
and all legal obligations toward the Buyer.

## 3. Authenticity & right to sell  *(core)*
The Seller **represents and warrants**, for every item listed, that:
- the goods are **genuine** (not counterfeit, replica, or imitation);
- the Seller is **authorised / legally entitled to sell** them in the destination market, including
  compliance with **trademark exhaustion** (goods first placed on the EEA market by/with the brand
  owner's consent) where applicable; and
- the goods are lawful, safe, and accurately described.
The Seller will provide **proof of authenticity / provenance / authorisation** on request.

## 4. Indemnification  *(core)*
The Seller will **indemnify and hold harmless** VESTRA from any claim, loss, fine, or cost
(including legal fees) arising from the Seller's goods, listings, breach of warranties, IP
infringement, or transactions.

## 5. IP & notice-and-takedown cooperation
The Seller will comply with VESTRA's IP & Anti-Counterfeit Policy, respond promptly to notices,
provide evidence when requested, and accept that listings may be removed pending resolution.

## 6. Orders, escrow & payouts
- Orders are fulfilled per the agreed terms; funds are held in **escrow** by the licensed provider
  and released to the Seller after Buyer confirmation / verified delivery.
- VESTRA deducts its **commission/fees** as published.

## 7. Strikes & suspension
Violations (counterfeit, IP infringement, repeated valid complaints, fraud) lead to listing
removal, **strikes**, and **suspension/termination**. Manifest counterfeit/fraud may result in
immediate suspension without prior strikes.

## 8. Term & termination
Either party may terminate per notice; obligations relating to liability, indemnity, and pending
orders survive.
