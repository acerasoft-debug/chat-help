# VESTRA — IP & Anti-Counterfeit / Notice-and-Takedown Policy

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Central to operating a branded-goods marketplace lawfully and to
> passing payment-provider review. Lawyer review recommended.

## 1. Zero tolerance
VESTRA prohibits counterfeit, replica, unauthorised-brand, and unverified grey-market goods, and
any listing that infringes a third party's intellectual property (IP) rights.

## 2. Seller obligations
Sellers warrant goods are genuine and that they are entitled to sell them (see Seller Agreement),
and must provide proof of authenticity / authorisation / EEA exhaustion on request.

## 3. Reporting an infringement (rights holders & users)
Send a notice to **[ip@vestra-domain.com]** including:
- the right relied on (e.g., trademark registration number) and proof of ownership/authority;
- the **exact listing URL(s)** concerned;
- the reason the listing is infringing; and
- a good-faith statement and contact details.
A single notice may list multiple URLs. Generic "remove everything" requests without specific
listings cannot be actioned.

## 4. Our process
1. Acknowledge receipt.
2. Assess the notice; for substantiated/manifest cases, **remove or disable** the listing promptly.
3. Notify the Seller with the reason.
4. Allow the Seller to **counter-notice** with evidence (authenticity, authorisation, EEA proof).
5. Reinstate only if the Seller provides sufficient proof and the complaint is not maintained;
   when in doubt, the listing stays down.

## 5. Repeat infringers & stay-down
- A **strike** system applies; repeated valid complaints lead to **suspension**.
- For **specific identified** infringing items, VESTRA takes reasonable measures to prevent
  re-listing (**stay-down**), including filtering and seller suspension.
- Manifest counterfeit/fraud may result in immediate removal and suspension.

## 6. Trusted rights-holder channel
Brand owners and their agents may request a priority reporting channel. VESTRA cooperates with
rights holders while preserving a fair counter-notice/appeal process for Sellers (consistent with
EU DSA duties).

## 7. Misuse of notices
Repeated unfounded or abusive notices may lead to restriction of the notifier.
