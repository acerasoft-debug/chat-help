# VESTRA — Prohibited & Restricted Items

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Aligns with payment-provider and EU rules; refine as needed.

## Prohibited (never allowed)
- **Counterfeit, replica, or imitation** goods of any kind.
- **Unauthorised-brand** goods, or goods the Seller is **not legally entitled to sell** in the
  destination market (including unverified **grey-market / parallel imports** without proof of
  EEA exhaustion or authorisation).
- Stolen, smuggled, or illegally sourced goods.
- Goods infringing any **intellectual property** right.
- Illegal items, weapons, drugs, hazardous/recalled products, or anything unlawful to sell.

## Restricted (allowed only with conditions / proof)
- **Branded goods** — require seller verification and, on request, **proof of authenticity /
  authorisation / provenance**.
- Categories subject to **safety/labelling** rules (e.g., textiles fibre-composition labelling) —
  must comply with applicable regulations (e.g., EU GPSR, textile labelling).

## Enforcement
Violations lead to **listing removal, strikes, and suspension/termination**, and may be reported
to rights holders and authorities. Manifest counterfeit/fraud → **immediate suspension**.
