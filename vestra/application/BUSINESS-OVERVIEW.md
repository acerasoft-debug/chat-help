## 2. What VESTRA is
VESTRA is a **B2B (business-to-business) wholesale marketplace** for **branded and textile
fashion** (apparel, footwear, accessories, basics). It connects **verified wholesale sellers**
with **verified business buyers** (boutiques, retailers, online resellers) across Europe and
worldwide.

VESTRA is an **intermediary platform** — it is **not a party** to the sales contracts between
sellers and buyers, does not take title to goods, and does not hold customer funds. It provides
the storefront, verification, and a secured (escrow) ordering experience.

## 3. Who uses VESTRA
- **Sellers:** verified wholesale businesses (KYB-checked). Only approved, identified business
  sellers may list. No anonymous or consumer sellers.
- **Buyers:** verified businesses purchasing wholesale quantities.
- **Geography:** buyers/sellers primarily in the EU/EEA; expanding worldwide.

## 4. Revenue model
- **Membership / subscription** and/or **commission** on completed orders.
- VESTRA invoices its **own fee** only; it does **not** collect or redistribute third-party funds
  itself (that is handled by the licensed payment/escrow provider).

## 5. Payments & escrow (why funds are handled safely)
- Buyer payment is collected and **held in escrow by the licensed provider** (Tazapay — MAS /
  US licensed). VESTRA never custodies the funds.
- Funds are **released to the seller only after** the buyer confirms receipt, or delivery is
  verified, per the agreed terms. VESTRA's platform triggers release via the provider's API.
- Methods: **SEPA bank transfer** (primary for EU B2B) and cards, via the provider.
- No chargeback exposure on bank-transfer rails; disputes handled through the provider + VESTRA's
  dispute process.

## 6. Risk & compliance controls (key for branded goods)
- **Seller KYB/KYC:** business registration, VAT/tax ID, UBO identity — every seller verified.
- **Authenticity-first:** sellers warrant goods are **genuine and that they hold the right to
  sell** them in the destination market (including EEA exhaustion / authorization); proof on request.
- **Prohibited:** counterfeit, replica, unauthorized-brand and unverified grey-market goods
  (see Prohibited Items Policy).
- **Notice-and-takedown:** rights holders can report infringing listings; rapid removal,
  seller counter-notice, **repeat-infringer suspension**, and **stay-down** for identified items
  (see IP & Anti-Counterfeit Policy).
- **AML / sanctions:** screening and ongoing monitoring (see AML/KYC Policy); fund handling and
  settlement performed by the licensed provider.

## 7. Why VESTRA is a lower-risk marketplace
- Closed, **verified-only** network (no anonymous sellers/buyers).
- **B2B** (no consumer-facing impulse sales) with documented trade relationships.
- Funds flow through a **licensed escrow provider**, not the platform.
- Active **IP/anti-counterfeit** program aligned with brand owners and EU DSA/GPSR duties.
